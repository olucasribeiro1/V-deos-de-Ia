# lie_head - "In this town, every time you lie, your head grows one block bigger." Lead: Kai (blocktopia.kai_).
# Hook (result first: Kai's giant head stuck in the school door) -> rule -> 3 examples (sister, mayor, dog) ->
# "except me" pivot -> ladder of lies (dog ate it, dinosaur, dragon, "it's not that big") -> stuck ->
# "Then I noticed something" (truth shrinks it) -> confessions -> payoff -> button (one more lie: Bonk).
In this town, I told one tiny lie, and now my head is stuck in the school door!
Because in this town, every time you lie, your head grows one block bigger!
Everybody knows it! My little sister said she didn't eat the cookies. Boop! Her head grew.
The mayor said he'd fix every road. Now his head is bigger than his car!
The weatherman promised sunshine. Now his head blocks the sun!
Even my dog has a big head! Don't ask!
So in this town, everyone tells the truth. Everyone... except me.
This morning, I forgot my homework. My teacher looked at me. "Kai, where is your homework?"
And I said... "My dog ate it." Bonk! My head grew one block. The whole class gasped!
"I mean, a dinosaur ate it!" Bonk! Bigger!
"A dinosaur riding a dragon!" Bonk! Bonk! Bonk!
Now my head was as big as a fridge. My headphones snapped! Everybody was laughing.
"It's not that big!" I yelled. Bonk! My head smashed right through the ceiling!
Birds landed in my hair. An airplane had to turn around!
My best friend took a selfie with my giant head. A million likes!
I tried to run home, but I got stuck in the school door.
The principal came running. "Kai, just tell the truth!"
But I was scared. If I told the truth, I'd be in SO much trouble!
Then I noticed something. My sister said sorry for the cookies, and her head shrank back. Pop!
The truth makes it smaller!
So I took a deep breath. "I didn't do my homework. I played video games all night." Pop!
"And I ate the last slice of pizza." Pop!
"And I'm the one who broke the window!" Pop! Pop! Pop!
My head was normal again. Everybody cheered! Even my sister gave me a cookie.
My teacher smiled. "Thank you for the truth, Kai. Now you have double homework."
And I said, "I love homework!"
Bonk!
